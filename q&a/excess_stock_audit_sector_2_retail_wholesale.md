# Excess Stock Audit Framework
## Sector 2: Retail & Wholesale

This document defines the **Excess Stock Audit Question Framework** for the Retail & Wholesale sector.

It includes:
- Short Form Trigger Questions
- Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Calculation Logic per Subcategory

All questions focus on **overstocking, slow-moving goods, obsolete inventory, damaged products, seasonal leftovers, and storage inefficiencies**.

---

## SECTOR: Retail & Wholesale

### Categories Covered
1. Grocery & Convenience
2. Fashion & Accessories
3. Electronics & Appliances
4. Home & Lifestyle
5. Wholesale & Distribution

---

# 1. Grocery & Convenience

## Subcategories
- Supermarkets
- Convenience Stores
- Off-Licences
- Mini-Marts

---

## Short Form Trigger Questions (Grocery)

1. What percentage of perishable goods expires monthly?
2. How often are unsold fresh items discarded?
3. Do promotions lead to excess inventory?
4. What value of expired packaged food is written off?
5. How much chilled stock is wasted weekly?
6. Do supplier minimum orders cause overstocking?
7. How often do seasonal items remain unsold?
8. What percentage of damaged packaging is discarded?
9. Do you track daily wastage?
10. How much shelf stock remains unsold after 90 days?

---

## Long Form Deep-Dive Questions (Grocery)

1. Monthly expired stock value (£)?
2. Percentage of short-dated stock?
3. Supplier return rejection rate?
4. Overstock from promotions value?
5. Slow-moving branded goods cost?
6. Chilled/frozen spoilage rate?
7. Overstocked private label products?
8. Demand forecast accuracy?
9. Shrinkage vs expiry comparison?
10. Storage capacity utilisation?
11. Waste reduction system in use?
12. Bulk buying inefficiency cost?
13. Markdown effectiveness rate?
14. Clearance stock turnover?
15. Expiry rotation compliance?

---

## Calculation Logic (Grocery)

Total Excess =
Expired + Spoiled + Slow-Moving + Damaged + Unsold Promotional Stock

Excess % =
(Total Excess ÷ Monthly Stock Purchases) × 100

Risk Rating:
0–5% Excellent | 6–10% Low | 11–20% Moderate | 21%+ High

# Sector 2 Output Summary

For all Retail & Wholesale subcategories, the system generates:

- Total Excess Stock Value (£)
- Excess Stock Percentage
- Risk Classification
- Key Loss Sources
- Inventory Optimisation Priorities
- Cash Recovery Opportunities

This output feeds into the recommendation, monetisation, and advisory modules.
