# Excess Stock Audit Framework
## Sector 8: Transport, Logistics & Automotive

This document defines the **Excess Stock Audit Question Framework** for the Transport, Logistics & Automotive sector.

Excess stock in this sector can include **vehicle parts, tyres, lubricants, automotive tools, office supplies, packaging materials, logistics equipment, and promotional items**.

It includes:
- Short Form Trigger Questions
- Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Calculation Logic per Subcategory

# 1. Passenger Transport

## Subcategories: Taxi Firms, Private Hire, Minibus Operators, Coach Services

### Short Form Trigger Questions
1. Are vehicle spare parts overstocked?
2. Are cleaning/maintenance supplies idle?
3. Are office supplies over-purchased?
4. Are cancelled bookings leaving surplus stock?
5. Are seasonal vehicle items accumulating?
6. Are promotional materials unused?
7. Are PPE/hygiene materials overstocked?
8. Are vendor minimum order items creating surplus?
9. Are consumables (e.g., fuel additives, oils) expiring unused?
10. Is storage overfilled?

### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
1. Value of overstocked spare parts (£)?
2. Idle cleaning/maintenance supplies cost?
3. Office supplies overage?
4. Cancelled booking surplus stock?
5. Seasonal vehicle item accumulation?
6. Idle promotional materials?
7. PPE/hygiene overstock?
8. Vendor bulk order impact?
9. Expired consumables cost?
10. Storage inefficiency?
11. Forecast vs actual usage?
12. Theft/misplacement cost?
13. Redundant administrative stock?
14. Resource utilisation efficiency?
15. Vehicle downtime due to excess/idle stock?

### Calculation Logic
Total Excess = Spare Parts + Cleaning/Maintenance Supplies + Office Supplies + Promotional Materials + PPE + Consumables

Excess % = (Total Excess ÷ Total Operational & Material Investment) × 100
