# Excess Stock Audit Framework
## Sector 1: Hospitality, Food & Beverage

This document defines the **Excess Stock Audit Question Framework** for the Hospitality, Food & Beverage sector.

It includes:
- Short Form Trigger Questions
- Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Calculation Logic per Subcategory

These questions focus strictly on **inventory, wastage, overstocking, storage inefficiencies, and expired/slow-moving stock**.

## Excess Stock Calculation Logic (Restaurants)

### Input Variables
- Monthly food purchases (£)
- Monthly waste value (£)
- Expired stock value (£)
- Slow-moving stock value (£)
- Unsold prepared food value (£)
- Damaged stock value (£)

### Formula

Total Excess Stock Value =
Waste + Expired + Slow-Moving + Unsold + Damaged Stock

Excess Stock Percentage =
(Total Excess Stock Value ÷ Monthly Purchases) × 100

### Risk Rating
- 0–5% = Excellent Control
- 6–10% = Low Risk
- 11–20% = Moderate Risk
- 21%+ = High Risk
