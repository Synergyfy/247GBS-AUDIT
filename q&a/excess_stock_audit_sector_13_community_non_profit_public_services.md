# Excess Stock Audit Framework
## Sector 13: Community, Non-Profit & Public Services

This document defines the **Excess Stock Audit Question Framework** for the Community, Non-Profit & Public Services sector.

Excess stock in this sector can include **donated goods, food supplies, clothing, office supplies, printed materials, equipment, welfare items, and PPE**.

It includes:
- Short Form Trigger Questions
- Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Calculation Logic per Subcategory

---

## SECTOR: Community, Non-Profit & Public Services

### Categories & Subcategories
1. Charities & NGOs
   - Relief Organisations
   - Community Trusts
   - Foundations
2. Religious Organisations

   - Mosques

3. Public & Social Services
   - Care Homes

4. Associations & Membership Bodies
   - Trade Associations
   - Chambers of Commerce
   - Professional Bodies

# 1. Charities & NGOs

### Short Form Trigger Questions
1. Are donated goods overstocked?
2. Are food or relief supplies unused?
3. Are clothing or household items accumulating?
4. Are office supplies idle?
5. Are printed materials over-produced?
6. Are seasonal donations piling up?
7. Are storage areas overcrowded?
8. Are expired welfare items present?
9. Are PPE/hygiene items overstocked?
10. Are equipment items underutilised?

### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
1. Value of unused donated goods (£)?
2. Expired food/relief supplies cost?
3. Idle clothing/household items value?
4. Office supply surplus?
5. Over-printed awareness materials cost?
6. Seasonal donation accumulation?
7. Storage inefficiency losses?
8. Waste disposal cost from expired items?
9. PPE overstock cost?
10. Underutilised equipment value?
11. Forecast vs actual donation volume?
12. Misplaced/lost donation value?
13. Administrative stock redundancy?
14. Distribution inefficiency cost?
15. Operational inefficiency due to excess stock?

### Calculation Logic
Total Excess = Donated Goods + Food/Relief Supplies + Clothing/Household Items + Office Supplies + Printed Materials + PPE + Equipment

Excess % = (Total Excess ÷ Total Donation & Operational Investment) × 100
