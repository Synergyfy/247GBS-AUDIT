# Excess Stock Audit Framework
## Sector 5: Construction, Property & Trades

This document defines the **Excess Stock Audit Question Framework** for the Construction, Property & Trades sector.

Excess stock in this sector includes **construction materials, tools, PPE, office supplies, promotional items, and equipment**.

It includes:
- Short Form Trigger Questions
- Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Calculation Logic per Subcategory

# 1. Construction

## Subcategory: Residential Builders

### Short Form Trigger Questions
1. Are construction materials overstocked?
2. Are tools idle or unused?
3. Are PPE items over-purchased?
4. Are cancelled or delayed projects leaving surplus?
5. Are seasonal equipment or materials accumulating?
6. Are office supplies overstocked?
7. Are promotional materials idle?
8. Are subcontractor supplies unused?
9. Are vendor minimum order items creating surplus?
10. Are storage facilities overfilled?

### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
1. Value of overstocked construction materials (£)?
2. Idle tools and equipment cost?
3. PPE overage cost?
4. Cancelled/delayed project material surplus?
5. Seasonal equipment/material accumulation?
6. Office supplies overage?
7. Idle promotional materials?
8. Unused subcontractor supplies?
9. Vendor bulk order impact?
10. Storage inefficiency cost?
11. Forecast vs actual material usage?
12. Expired or obsolete materials?
13. Theft/misplacement cost?
14. Redundant administrative stock?
15. Resource utilisation efficiency?

### Calculation Logic
Total Excess = Construction Materials + Tools + PPE + Office Supplies + Promotional Materials + Subcontractor Supplies

Excess % = (Total Excess ÷ Total Operational & Material Investment) × 100
