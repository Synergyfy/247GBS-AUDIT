# Excess Stock Audit Framework
## Sector 9: Technology, Digital & Creative

This document defines the **Excess Stock Audit Question Framework** for the Technology, Digital & Creative sector.

Excess stock in this sector can include **hardware, office supplies, digital assets, software licenses, promotional items, creative tools, and consumables**.

It includes:
- Short Form Trigger Questions
- Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Calculation Logic per Subcategory

---

## SECTOR: Technology, Digital & Creative

### Categories & Subcategories
1. Software & IT
   - Software Developers
   - SaaS Providers
   - IT Support Firms

2. Web & Design

# 1. Software & IT

### Short Form Trigger Questions
1. Are hardware items (servers, computers, peripherals) overstocked?
2. Are software licenses underutilised?
3. Are office supplies idle?
4. Are digital assets over-purchased?
5. Are cancelled projects leaving surplus materials?
6. Are promotional materials unused?
7. Are vendor minimum order items creating surplus?
8. Are consumables (e.g., backup drives, cables) expiring unused?
9. Are PPE or hygiene materials overstocked?
10. Is storage (physical or digital) overfilled?

### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
1. Value of overstocked hardware (£)?
2. Underutilised software licenses cost?
3. Idle office supplies cost?
4. Cancelled project surplus stock?
5. Digital asset overage cost?
6. Idle promotional materials?
7. Vendor bulk order impact?
8. Expired consumables cost?
9. PPE/hygiene overstock?
10. Storage inefficiency?
11. Forecast vs actual usage?
12. Theft/misplacement cost?
13. Redundant administrative stock?
14. Resource utilisation efficiency?
15. Operational inefficiency due to excess stock?

### Calculation Logic
Total Excess = Hardware + Software Licenses + Office Supplies + Digital Assets + Promotional Materials + Consumables + PPE

Excess % = (Total Excess ÷ Total Operational & Material Investment) × 100
