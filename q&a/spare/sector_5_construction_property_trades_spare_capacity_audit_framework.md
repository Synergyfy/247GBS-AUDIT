# Sector 5 – Construction, Property & Trades
## Spare Capacity Audit Framework (247 GBS Audit Platform)

---

## 1. Sector Overview
This framework evaluates unused labour, equipment, site capacity, scheduling efficiency, and service delivery potential across construction, property services, trades, and facilities management businesses.

---

## 2. Categories, Subcategories & Question Frameworks

---

# A. Construction

## 1. Residential Builders
### Short Form Trigger Questions
- Average site utilisation rate (%)
- Idle worker days per month
- Unused equipment on sites
- Project gaps between builds
- Delayed material delivery time

### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Planning approval delays
- Contractor scheduling inefficiencies
- Weather disruption impact
- Subcontractor availability gaps
- Rework and defect frequency

### Capacity Calculation Logic
Unused Capacity = (Idle Labour Days × Daily Margin) + (Idle Equipment Hours × Hire Value)

## 3. Recommendation Engine Logic (Sector-Wide)

- 0–20% unused: Scheduling optimisation
- 21–40%: Capacity monetisation
- 41–60%: Market expansion
- 61%+: Structural restructuring
