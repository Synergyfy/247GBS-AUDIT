# Sector 2 – Retail & Wholesale
## Spare Capacity Audit Framework

This framework identifies unused operational, staffing, space, inventory handling, and sales capacity within Retail & Wholesale businesses and converts it into revenue opportunities.

---

## 1. Grocery & Convenience

### Supermarkets

#### Short Form Trigger Questions
- Are there shelves, aisles, or fridges regularly underused?
- Do you experience quiet hours with low customer traffic?
- Are staff idle during off-peak periods?
- Is storage space partially empty?

#### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- What percentage of trading hours have low footfall?
- How much refrigerated/frozen space is unused weekly?
- How many staff hours are unproductive per week?
- Which product sections rotate slowly?
- How often do promotional spaces remain empty?

#### Calculation Logic
Spare Capacity Value = (Idle Staff Hours × Average Hourly Margin) + (Unused Shelf Space × Revenue per Sq Ft) + (Unused Cold Storage × Avg Product Margin)

---

### Convenience Stores / Off-Licences / Mini-Marts

#### Short Form Trigger Questions
- Do you have unused shelf or counter space?
- Are late-night/early-morning hours slow?
- Do fridges operate below capacity?

#### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Which time slots generate less than 50% of peak sales?
- How many shelves remain partially stocked?
- What percentage of fridge space is unused?
- How many hours per week staff are waiting for customers?

#### Calculation Logic
Spare Capacity = (Idle Hours × Avg Sales per Hour) + (Unused Display Units × Avg Product Profit)

---

## 2. Fashion & Accessories

### Clothing Stores / Shoe Shops / Jewellery / Bag & Luggage / Tailors

#### Short Form Trigger Questions
- Do fitting rooms remain unused most days?
- Are mannequins and displays underutilised?
- Are tailoring machines idle?

#### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- What percentage of floor space generates low sales?
- How many fitting room hours are unused?
- How often are tailors waiting for jobs?
- Which racks have low turnover?

#### Calculation Logic
Spare Capacity = (Unused Floor Area × Sales per Sq Ft) + (Idle Tailor Hours × Service Margin)

---

## 3. Electronics & Appliances

### Mobile Phone / Computer / Appliance / Gaming Stores

#### Short Form Trigger Questions
- Do demo stations remain unused?
- Are technicians idle?
- Is repair/workshop space empty?

#### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- How many demo units go unused daily?
- What percentage of repair bay time is idle?
- How much display space is inactive?
- Which service packages are under-promoted?

#### Calculation Logic
Spare Capacity = (Idle Repair Hours × Repair Margin) + (Unused Display Space × Avg Product Profit)

---

## 4. Home & Lifestyle

### Furniture / Home Décor / Lighting / Kitchenware Stores

#### Short Form Trigger Questions
- Do showrooms have low-traffic areas?
- Are delivery vehicles underused?
- Is warehouse space partly empty?

#### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Which display zones generate low sales?
- How often are delivery vans below capacity?
- What percentage of storage space is unused?
- How many staff hours are idle?

#### Calculation Logic
Spare Capacity = (Unused Showroom Area × Revenue per Sq Ft) + (Idle Delivery Capacity × Avg Delivery Profit)

---

## 5. Wholesale & Distribution

### Cash & Carry / Trade Wholesalers / Import-Export / Distribution Centres

#### Short Form Trigger Questions
- Are pallets or racking spaces empty?
- Are loading bays underused?
- Are vehicles idle?

#### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- What percentage of warehouse capacity is unused?
- How many loading hours are idle weekly?
- How many vehicles operate below capacity?
- Which routes are underutilised?

#### Calculation Logic
Spare Capacity = (Unused Storage Space × Storage Revenue Rate) + (Idle Vehicle Hours × Transport Margin) + (Unused Loading Time × Handling Fee)

---

## Output for Recommendation Engine

For each subcategory, system should generate:
- Identified idle assets (space, staff, equipment, vehicles)
- Monetisation options (subletting, shared logistics, pop-ups, promotions, services)
- Priority ranking (High / Medium / Low)
- Monthly revenue recovery estimate
