# Sector 4 – Professional & Business Services
## Spare Capacity Audit Framework (247 GBS Audit Platform)

---

## 1. Sector Overview
This framework measures unused professional time, service delivery capacity, client management efficiency, and operational bandwidth across advisory, legal, financial, marketing, and recruitment organisations.

---

## 2. Categories, Subcategories & Question Frameworks

## 1. Management Consulting
### Short Form Trigger Questions
- Average consultant utilisation rate (%)
- Unbilled hours per week
- Idle project capacity
- Client pipeline gaps

- Project scheduling inefficiencies
- Consultant skill mismatches
- Delays in client approvals
- Underpricing impact
- Resource allocation gaps

Unused Capacity = (Available Consultant Hours – Billable Hours)

## 3. Recommendation Engine Logic (Sector-Wide)

- 0–20% unused: Efficiency tuning
- 21–40%: Client acquisition focus
- 41–60%: Service diversification
- 61%+: Structural realignment
