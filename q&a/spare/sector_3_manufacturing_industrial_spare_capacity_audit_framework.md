# Sector 3 – Manufacturing & Industrial
## Spare Capacity Audit Framework (247 GBS Audit Platform)

---

## 1. Sector Overview
This framework evaluates unused production, machine, labour, and facility capacity across manufacturing and industrial operations. It identifies underutilised assets and translates them into recoverable revenue opportunities.

---

## 2. Categories, Subcategories & Question Frameworks

---

# A. Light Manufacturing

### 1. Clothing Manufacturing
#### Short Form Trigger Questions
- Average machine utilisation rate (%)
- Idle sewing/finishing hours per week
- Unused production line capacity
- Backlog vs available capacity
- Staff idle time per shift

#### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Seasonal demand fluctuations impact
- Bottlenecks in cutting/sewing/finishing
- Rejected or delayed orders
- Stockpiling due to slow output
- Outsourcing vs in-house imbalance

#### Capacity Calculation Logic
Available Capacity = (Total Machine Hours – Used Hours) + (Idle Labour Hours)

## 3. Recommendation Engine Logic (Sector-Wide)

- 0–20% unused: Optimisation tuning
- 21–40%: Revenue activation
- 41–60%: Market expansion
- 61%+: Structural redesign

---

## 4. Output Metrics

- Recoverable production value
- Idle asset monetisation
- Labour redeployment potential
- ROI from capacity activation
- Risk of overcapacity
