# Sector 1 – Hospitality, Food & Beverage
## Spare Capacity Audit Framework

This document defines structured Spare Capacity Questions for businesses in the Hospitality, Food & Beverage sector. These questions help identify underused time, staff, equipment, space, systems, and market potential in order to design targeted Recommended Solutions.

---

# 1. Restaurants & Dining

## 1.1 Fine Dining Restaurants

### Spare Capacity Questions
- What percentage of tables are empty during weekday lunches and early evenings?
- How many staff hours are paid for when customer volume is low?
- How often are private rooms or premium seating unused?
- Are kitchen stations idle outside peak dinner periods?
- How many reservation slots remain unfilled weekly?
- Are tasting menus or chef specials under-promoted?
- How often is equipment used below capacity?

## 1.2 Casual Dining Restaurants

### Spare Capacity Questions
- Which days and times have the lowest footfall?
- How many tables are unused during off-peak periods?
- Are staff shifts matched to real demand?
- Is delivery or takeaway underutilised?
- Are loyalty schemes fully exploited?
- Are function spaces regularly vacant?

## 1.3 Fast Food / QSR

### Spare Capacity Questions
- What are queue levels versus staff levels during slow hours?
- How many machines remain idle daily?
- Are drive-through or delivery services underused?
- Are late-night hours profitable?
- How often is prep capacity unused?

## 1.4 Takeaway Shops

### Spare Capacity Questions
- Which hours generate the lowest orders?
- Is delivery radius fully exploited?
- Are cooking stations idle during daytime?
- Is online ordering optimised?
- Are combo meals underused?

## 1.5 Street Food Vendors

### Spare Capacity Questions
- Which locations perform below potential?
- How many trading hours are missed weekly?
- Is equipment idle between events?
- Are catering opportunities pursued?
- Is social media reach underutilised?

## 1.6 Ghost Kitchens

### Spare Capacity Questions
- How many kitchen hours are unused daily?
- Are all delivery platforms fully utilised?
- How many menu brands are underperforming?
- Is packaging capacity underused?
- Are data insights fully applied?

## 1.7 Ethnic / Specialty Restaurants

### Spare Capacity Questions
- Are non-cultural peak days underutilised?
- Is niche marketing maximised?
- Are private events offered?
- Is menu diversification limited?
- Are catering opportunities explored?
