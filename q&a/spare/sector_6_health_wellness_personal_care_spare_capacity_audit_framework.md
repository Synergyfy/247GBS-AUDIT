# Sector 6 – Health, Wellness & Personal Care
## Spare Capacity Audit Framework (247 GBS Audit Platform)

---

## 1. Sector Overview
This framework evaluates unused clinical time, treatment rooms, practitioner availability, equipment utilisation, and client booking efficiency across health, wellness, fitness, and personal care services.

---

## 2. Categories, Subcategories & Question Frameworks

---

# A. Medical & Clinical

## 1. GP Clinics
### Short Form Trigger Questions
- Appointment slot utilisation rate (%)
- Missed or cancelled appointments per week
- Idle consultation rooms
- Clinician downtime
- Patient backlog

### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Scheduling inefficiencies
- Patient flow bottlenecks
- Referral processing delays
- Administrative overload
- Digital booking adoption gaps

### Capacity Calculation Logic
Unused Capacity = (Unused Appointment Slots × Avg Consultation Fee)

## 3. Recommendation Engine Logic (Sector-Wide)

- 0–20% unused: Booking optimisation
- 21–40%: Revenue activation
- 41–60%: Service diversification
- 61%+: Capacity restructuring
