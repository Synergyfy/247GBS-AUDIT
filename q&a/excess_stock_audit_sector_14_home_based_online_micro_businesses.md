# Excess Stock Audit Framework
## Sector 14: Home-Based, Online & Micro-Businesses

This document defines the **Excess Stock Audit Question Framework** for the Home-Based, Online & Micro-Businesses sector.

Excess stock in this sector can include **unsold inventory, packaging materials, office supplies, digital assets, promotional items, equipment, and consumables**.

It includes:
- Short Form Trigger Questions
- Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Calculation Logic per Subcategory

# 1. E-Commerce

### Short Form Trigger Questions
1. Do you have unsold products in storage?
2. Are packaging materials overstocked?
3. Are slow-moving items accumulating?
4. Are seasonal products left unsold?
5. Are cancelled orders creating surplus stock?
6. Are promotional items unused?
7. Are vendor bulk orders creating excess?
8. Are returned goods resalable?
9. Are damaged items piling up?
10. Is storage space overcrowded?

### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
1. Value of unsold inventory (£)?
2. Cost of slow-moving stock?
3. Seasonal unsold stock value?
4. Cancelled order surplus cost?
5. Overstocked packaging materials cost?
6. Returned goods recovery value?
7. Vendor minimum order impact?
8. Damaged stock losses?
9. Storage inefficiency cost?
10. Obsolete product write-offs?
11. Forecast vs actual sales variance?
12. Platform listing inefficiency losses?
13. Marketing-driven overstock risk?
14. Dead stock percentage?
15. Cash flow tied in excess stock?

### Calculation Logic
Total Excess = Unsold Inventory + Packaging Materials + Returned Goods + Damaged Stock + Obsolete Stock

Excess % = (Total Excess ÷ Total Inventory Investment) × 100
