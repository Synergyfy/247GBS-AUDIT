# Excess Stock Audit Framework
## Sector 12: Agriculture, Environment & Energy

This document defines the **Excess Stock Audit Question Framework** for the Agriculture, Environment & Energy sector.

Excess stock in this sector can include **fertilisers, seeds, feed, tools, machinery parts, office supplies, promotional materials, PPE, and consumables**.

It includes:
- Short Form Trigger Questions
- Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Calculation Logic per Subcategory

---

# 1. Farming & Food Production

### Short Form Trigger Questions
1. Are seeds or feed overstocked?
2. Are fertilisers or pesticides idle or over-purchased?
3. Are tools or machinery parts unused?
4. Are office supplies idle?
5. Are seasonal materials accumulating?
6. Are promotional materials unused?
7. Are vendor minimum order items creating surplus?
8. Are consumables (e.g., packaging, labelling) expiring unused?
9. Are PPE/hygiene materials overstocked?
10. Is storage overfilled?

### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
1. Value of overstocked seeds/feed/fertiliser (£)?
2. Idle tools/machinery parts cost?
3. Idle office supplies cost?
4. Cancelled production or harvest surplus stock?
5. Seasonal material accumulation?
6. Idle promotional materials?
7. Vendor bulk order impact?
8. Expired consumables cost?
9. PPE/hygiene overstock?
10. Storage inefficiency?
11. Forecast vs actual usage?
12. Theft/misplacement cost?
13. Redundant administrative stock?
14. Resource utilisation efficiency?
15. Operational inefficiency due to excess stock?

### Calculation Logic
Total Excess = Seeds/Feed/Fertiliser + Tools/Machinery Parts + Office Supplies + Promotional Materials + Consumables + PPE

Excess % = (Total Excess ÷ Total Operational & Material Investment) × 100
