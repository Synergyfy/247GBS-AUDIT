# Excess Stock Audit Framework
## Sector 6: Health, Wellness & Personal Care

This document defines the **Excess Stock Audit Question Framework** for the Health, Wellness & Personal Care sector.

Excess stock in this sector can include **medical supplies, clinical equipment, office supplies, promotional items, beauty and fitness products, wellness tools, and PPE**.

It includes:
- Short Form Trigger Questions
- Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Calculation Logic per Subcategory

---

# 1. Medical & Clinical

### Short Form Trigger Questions
1. Are medical/clinical supplies overstocked?
2. Are office supplies idle?
3. Are PPE items over-purchased?
4. Are cancelled appointments leaving surplus stock?
5. Are seasonal supplies accumulating?
6. Are promotional materials unused?
7. Are medical devices or equipment underutilised?
8. Are vendor minimum order items creating surplus?
9. Are pharmaceuticals or consumables expiring unused?
10. Is storage overfilled?

### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
1. Value of overstocked medical/clinical supplies (£)?
2. Idle office supplies cost?
3. PPE overage cost?
4. Cancelled appointment surplus stock?
5. Seasonal supplies accumulation?
6. Idle promotional materials?
7. Underutilised medical devices/equipment?
8. Vendor bulk order impact?
9. Expired pharmaceuticals/consumables cost?
10. Storage inefficiency?
11. Forecast vs actual usage?
12. Theft/misplacement cost?
13. Redundant administrative stock?
14. Resource utilisation efficiency?
15. Compliance risk due to excess stock?

### Calculation Logic
Total Excess = Medical/Clinical Supplies + PPE + Office Supplies + Promotional Materials + Equipment + Consumables

Excess % = (Total Excess ÷ Total Operational & Material Investment) × 100
