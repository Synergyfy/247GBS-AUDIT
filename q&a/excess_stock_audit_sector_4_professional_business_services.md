# Excess Stock Audit Framework
## Sector 4: Professional & Business Services

This document defines the **Excess Stock Audit Question Framework** for the Professional & Business Services sector.

Although service-based, this sector still generates excess stock in the form of **unused subscriptions, software licences, office supplies, marketing materials, training resources, documentation, and idle digital assets**.

It includes:
- Short Form Trigger Questions
- Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Calculation Logic per Subcategory

---

## SECTOR: Professional & Business Services

### Categories Covered
1. Consulting & Advisory
2. Legal & Compliance
3. Accounting & Finance
4. Marketing & Media
5. Recruitment & Training

---

# 1. Consulting & Advisory

## Subcategories
- Management Consulting
- Financial Consulting
- HR Consulting
- Strategy Advisory

---

## Short Form Trigger Questions (Consulting)

1. How many unused software licences are paid for monthly?
2. What value of unused office supplies exists?
3. Do unused research reports accumulate?
4. How much training material is outdated?
5. Are marketing brochures overprinted?
6. Do client cancellations create wasted resources?
7. How many inactive digital tools are subscribed?
8. Is unused data storage paid for?
9. Do unused templates accumulate?
10. Is archived material still incurring costs?

---

## Long Form Deep-Dive Questions (Consulting)

1. Annual unused subscription cost (£)?
2. Redundant software overlap value?
3. Outdated research write-offs?
4. Storage inefficiency cost?
5. Overprinted marketing collateral losses?
6. Cancelled project resource waste?
7. Underutilised analytics tools?
8. Licence utilisation percentage?
9. Duplicate platform subscriptions?
10. Vendor lock-in wastage?
11. Knowledge base obsolescence rate?
12. Unused cloud capacity cost?
13. Legacy system maintenance cost?
14. Idle project tools expense?
15. Third-party report redundancy?

---

## Calculation Logic (Consulting)

Total Excess =
Unused Licences + Redundant Subscriptions + Obsolete Materials + Idle Digital Assets + Wasted Print

Excess % =
(Total Excess ÷ Total Operating Software & Resource Cost) × 100
