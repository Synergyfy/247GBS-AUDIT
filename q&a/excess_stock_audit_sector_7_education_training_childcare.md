# Excess Stock Audit Framework
## Sector 7: Education, Training & Childcare

This document defines the **Excess Stock Audit Question Framework** for the Education, Training & Childcare sector.

Excess stock in this sector can include **learning materials, stationery, educational equipment, digital assets, promotional items, and office supplies**.

It includes:
- Short Form Trigger Questions
- Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Calculation Logic per Subcategory

# 1. Schools & Colleges

## Subcategories: Private Schools, Tutorial Colleges, Sixth Form Colleges

### Short Form Trigger Questions
1. Are textbooks or learning materials overstocked?
2. Are stationery items idle or over-purchased?
3. Are classroom equipment or resources underutilised?
4. Are cancelled classes or courses leaving surplus materials?
5. Are seasonal teaching aids accumulating?
6. Are promotional materials unused?
7. Are office supplies overstocked?
8. Are vendor minimum order items creating surplus?
9. Are consumables (e.g., lab materials, art supplies) expiring unused?
10. Is storage space overfilled?

### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
1. Value of overstocked learning materials (£)?
2. Idle stationery or teaching aids cost?
3. Underutilised classroom equipment cost?
4. Cancelled class/course surplus materials?
5. Seasonal material accumulation?
6. Idle promotional materials?
7. Office supplies overage?
8. Vendor bulk order impact?
9. Expired consumables cost?
10. Storage inefficiency?
11. Forecast vs actual usage?
12. Theft/misplacement cost?
13. Redundant administrative stock?
14. Resource utilisation efficiency?
15. Curriculum compliance risk due to excess stock?

### Calculation Logic
Total Excess = Learning Materials + Stationery + Classroom Equipment + Office Supplies + Promotional Materials + Consumables

Excess % = (Total Excess ÷ Total Operational & Material Investment) × 100
