# Excess Stock Audit Framework
## Sector 10: Financial, Insurance & Real Estate

This document defines the **Excess Stock Audit Question Framework** for the Financial, Insurance & Real Estate sector.

Excess stock in this sector can include **office supplies, IT equipment, promotional materials, financial forms, contracts, and legal documents**.

It includes:
- Short Form Trigger Questions
- Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Calculation Logic per Subcategory

---

## SECTOR: Financial, Insurance & Real Estate

### Categories & Subcategories
1. Financial Services
   - Financial Advisors
   - Mortgage Brokers
   - Investment Firms
   - Wealth Managers
2. Insurance Services
   - Insurance Brokers
   - Claims Handlers
   - Risk Consultants
3. Real Estate Development
   - Property Developers
   - Commercial Investors
   - Landlords
   - Housing Associations

---

# 1. Financial Services

### Short Form Trigger Questions
1. Are office supplies overstocked?
2. Are IT equipment or hardware underutilised?
3. Are promotional materials idle?
4. Are financial forms/contracts over-printed or unused?
5. Are cancelled client sessions leaving surplus materials?
6. Are seasonal or marketing materials accumulating?
7. Are vendor minimum order items creating surplus?
8. Are consumables (e.g., paper, ink) expiring unused?
9. Are PPE/hygiene materials overstocked?
10. Is storage overfilled?

### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
1. Value of idle office supplies (£)?
2. Underutilised IT equipment cost?
3. Idle promotional materials cost?
4. Cancelled client session surplus stock?
5. Over-printed financial forms/contracts cost?
6. Seasonal material accumulation?
7. Vendor bulk order impact?
8. Expired consumables cost?
9. PPE/hygiene overstock?
10. Storage inefficiency?
11. Forecast vs actual usage?
12. Theft/misplacement cost?
13. Redundant administrative stock?
14. Resource utilisation efficiency?
15. Operational inefficiency due to excess stock?

### Calculation Logic
Total Excess = Office Supplies + IT Equipment + Promotional Materials + Financial Forms/Contracts + Consumables + PPE

Excess % = (Total Excess ÷ Total Operational & Material Investment) × 100
