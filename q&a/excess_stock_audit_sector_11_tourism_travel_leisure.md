# Excess Stock Audit Framework
## Sector 11: Tourism, Travel & Leisure

This document defines the **Excess Stock Audit Question Framework** for the Tourism, Travel & Leisure sector.

Excess stock in this sector can include **guest amenities, promotional materials, office supplies, consumables, travel guides, event equipment, and seasonal items**.

It includes:
- Short Form Trigger Questions
- Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
- Calculation Logic per Subcategory

---

# 1. Accommodation

### Short Form Trigger Questions
1. Are guest amenities overstocked?
2. Are linens, towels, or bedding idle or over-purchased?
3. Are office supplies idle?
4. Are cancelled bookings leaving surplus materials?
5. Are seasonal decorations or items accumulating?
6. Are promotional materials unused?
7. Are vendor minimum order items creating surplus?
8. Are consumables (e.g., toiletries, cleaning products) expiring unused?
9. Are PPE/hygiene materials overstocked?
10. Is storage overfilled?

### Long Form Deep-Dive Questions
1. Value of overstocked guest amenities (£)?
2. Idle linens/bedding/towels cost?
3. Office supplies overage?
4. Cancelled booking surplus stock?
5. Seasonal items accumulation?
6. Idle promotional materials?
7. Vendor bulk order impact?
8. Expired consumables cost?
9. PPE/hygiene overstock?
10. Storage inefficiency?
11. Forecast vs actual usage?
12. Theft/misplacement cost?
13. Redundant administrative stock?
14. Resource utilisation efficiency?
15. Operational inefficiency due to excess stock?

### Calculation Logic
Total Excess = Guest Amenities + Linens/Towels/Bedding + Office Supplies + Promotional Materials + Consumables + PPE

Excess % = (Total Excess ÷ Total Operational & Material Investment) × 100
