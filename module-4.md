I think this is actually the module that Henry has spent **the most time talking about** across all the meetings.

This is also where I think many people misunderstand what Henry is trying to build.

Most audit systems stop at:

> You have Problem A, B and C.

Henry's system does **not** stop there.

He repeatedly says things like:

> "The audit has to do something."

> "It's solution-based."

> "There's a recommended solution that is in tune with your business."

> "Not generic AI."

> "Based on the budget."

> "Based on how quickly they want results."

This tells us that Module 4 is **not simply a recommendation page**.

It is a **Business Solution Engine**.

The Business Diagnosis (Module 3) identifies the problems.

The Solution Engine (Module 4) decides the best way to solve them.

That is a completely different responsibility.

---

# PURPOSE

The purpose of Module 4 is to convert the Business Diagnosis into a structured Business Improvement Plan.

Unlike traditional audit systems, this module does not simply list recommendations.

Instead, it:

* Prioritises issues.
* Matches each issue with the appropriate MCOM solution.
* Considers business complexity.
* Considers implementation priority.
* Considers budget.
* Considers desired time-to-results.
* Produces a phased implementation roadmap.

Henry repeatedly explained that the platform should recommend **the right solution for this business**, not generic business advice.

This helps the business owner understand the reasoning behind each recommendation before speaking with an Account Manager.

# Module 4 End State

At the completion of Module 4, the business owner has:

* Received a personalised Business Improvement Plan derived from their audit and diagnosis.
* Had recommendations refined using their implementation budget and desired timeframe.
* Seen each recommendation prioritised by urgency and business impact.
* Understood how each recommendation maps directly to a relevant MCOM ecosystem service.
* Received a phased implementation roadmap instead of a disconnected list of advice.
* Been prepared for the next stage, where an Account Manager reviews, validates, and converts the plan into a practical implementation proposal.

This module fulfills Henry's repeated requirement that the audit be **solution-based**, producing recommendations that are tailored to the business's sector, diagnosis, budget, and desired speed of implementation, rather than generic AI-generated suggestions.
