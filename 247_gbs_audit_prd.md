# 247GBS Audit System – Product Requirements Document

## Purpose
The Audit is used to find what a business is wasting and turn that waste into rewards that bring in paying customers.

The Audit looks at three things:
1. Excess Stock
2. Spare Capacity
3. Recommended Solution

The Audit is not for accounting. It is for turning unused products, time, and services into customer growth.

---

## Who Uses the Audit
- Every business that joins 247GBS must complete it
- It is filled out by the business owner or staff
- It is reviewed and used by the system to create rewards

---

## Part 1 – Business Basics
The Audit must first collect:
- Business name
- Business type (hotel, restaurant, shop, salon, gym, service, online, etc.)
- Location
- Days and hours they operate

This helps the system understand how the business works.

---

## Part 2 – Excess Stock
The business must list anything they already paid for that is not selling.

They should be asked:
- What products are sitting on shelves?
- What items are not selling well?
- What stock may expire?
- What services were paid for but not used?

For each item they enter:
- Name of product or service
- Normal selling price
- How many are available
- How long it normally takes to sell

The system stores this as reward inventory.

---

## Part 3 – Spare Capacity
The business must explain how much they could sell if fully booked.

They should be asked:
- How many customers can you serve per day?
- How many do you normally serve?
- What days are quiet?
- What time slots are empty?

For each service they enter:
- Type of service (room, seat, table, appointment, slot)
- How many exist
- How many are normally used
- What price a customer normally pays

This shows what is being wasted every day.

---

## Part 4 – Cost and Limits
The business must say:
- What it costs them to provide one unit
- The lowest price they can accept without losing money

This keeps rewards safe and fair.

---

## Part 5 – Reward Rules
The business must set:
- How much they are willing to give away as a reward
- What they must still be paid in cash

Example:
A hotel room worth $100 might allow $40 as reward and $60 must still be paid.

---

## Part 6 – Recommended Solution
Using the data, the system creates:
- What items should become rewards
- How much reward value each item should carry
- What price customers must still pay

This becomes:
- Gift cards
- Cashback offers
- Free add‑ons
- Bonus services

---

## Part 7 – Review Screen
Before saving, the business sees:
- All excess stock listed
- All spare capacity listed
- The rewards that will be created

They must confirm this is correct.

---

## Part 8 – Use of Audit Data
The audit data is used to:
- Create rewards in 247GBS
- Send offers into MCOM Rewards
- Power referrals and loyalty

The business cannot change reward values without updating the audit.

---

## Success Rule
If the audit is done correctly:
- No reward causes the business to lose money
- Unused stock and time become customers
- Rewards bring new and repeat buyers

This is the core engine of 247GBS.

---

## Screen-by-Screen User Flow

### Screen 1 – Welcome to Audit
Shows a simple message explaining that the Audit helps turn unused stock and empty time into new customers. Button: Start Audit.

### Screen 2 – Business Basics
Fields for business name, type, location, and opening days and hours. Button: Continue.

### Screen 3 – Excess Stock List
User can add items one by one. Each item asks for name, normal price, quantity, and how fast it usually sells. Button: Add Another or Continue.

### Screen 4 – Spare Capacity Overview
User enters how many customers they can serve per day and how many they usually serve. Then they select quiet days and times.

### Screen 5 – Spare Capacity Details
For each service they add type of service, total slots, used slots, and normal price. Button: Continue.

### Screen 6 – Cost and Limits
User enters what it costs them to provide one unit and the lowest price they can accept.

### Screen 7 – Reward Rules
User sets how much can be given as reward and how much must be paid in cash.

### Screen 8 – System Recommendation
The system shows which items will become rewards and the reward values.

### Screen 9 – Review and Confirm
Shows full summary of stock, capacity, and rewards. Button: Confirm Audit.

### Screen 10 – Audit Complete
Message that rewards will now appear inside 247GBS and MCOM Rewards.
