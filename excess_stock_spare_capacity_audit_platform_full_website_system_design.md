# Excess Stock & Spare Capacity Audit Platform

## 1. Purpose of the Platform
The Excess Stock & Spare Capacity platform is designed to help businesses **identify, measure, and monetise unused resources** within their operations. These resources may include:
- Idle staff time
- Underutilised equipment
- Empty seats, rooms, or space
- Excess or slow-moving inventory

The platform enables businesses to complete structured audits that calculate spare capacity and excess stock, then provides **practical, revenue-focused recommendations**.

The goal is clarity, simplicity, and action.

## 3. Audit Types Overview

### SHORT FORM AUDIT
The Short Form Audit is a **fast, self-service assessment**.

**Purpose**:
- Provide a high-level snapshot of spare capacity and excess stock
- Help businesses quickly understand where value is being lost

**Key Characteristics**:
- Independent, self-completed
- No AI or consultant guidance
- Limited number of essential questions
- Instant results

**Best For**:
- Quick evaluations
- Simple business models
- First-time users
- Businesses that want speed over depth

**Positioning**:
"A quick health check of your unused business capacity."

---

### LONG FORM AUDIT
The Long Form Audit is a **deep, guided business analysis**.

**Purpose**:
- Deliver a detailed understanding of spare capacity and excess stock
- Generate tailored, strategic recommendations

**Key Characteristics**:
- Extended question sets
- Sector- and niche-specific logic
- Optional AI assistant, consultant, or account manager support
- Scenario modelling and forecasting

**Best For**:
- Complex operations
- Multi-revenue businesses
- Seasonal or fluctuating demand
- Strategic planning and growth optimisation

**Positioning**:
"A full operational audit designed to unlock hidden revenue."

## 12. Step-by-Step Audit Flow

### Step 1: Spare Capacity Input

Users enter details such as:
- Idle staff
- Empty seats
- Unused equipment

**UI Features:**
- Up/down arrows for easy adjustment
- Real-time calculations

**System Action:**
- Calculates total spare capacity percentage

---

### Step 2: Excess Stock Input

Users enter:
- Quantity of excess stock
- Storage utilisation
- Waste levels

**UI Features:**
- Adjustable numeric inputs
- Instant feedback

**System Action:**
- Calculates excess stock value and impact

---

### Step 3: Recommended Solution

Based on combined results:
- Spare capacity percentage
- Excess stock levels

**System Output:**
- Capacity improvement suggestion
- Example: increase utilisation by 5%
- Auto-calculated projected value increase
