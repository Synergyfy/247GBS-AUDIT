I actually think **Module 3 is the most important module in the entire Audit Platform**, because this is where Henry repeatedly said **our current system is wrong**.

Across multiple meetings, Henry kept saying variations of:

> "The audit has to do something."

> "It's a means to an end."

> "It has to recommend solutions."

> "It has to produce something that's actually useful."

That means **Module 3 is not an "Audit Report."**

It is a **Business Diagnosis Platform**.

The audit questions are finished.

Now the system behaves like a business consultant.

This is where the platform begins to demonstrate its intelligence.

# PURPOSE

The purpose of Module 3 is to transform the audit responses into an understandable business diagnosis.

This module answers the business owner's first question:

> **"What is happening inside my business?"**

It does **not** yet answer:

> "What should I buy?"

That comes in Module 4.

Instead, Module 3 explains:

* What is working well.
* What is not working.
* Where the opportunities are.
* Which problems are affecting growth.
* Which issues require immediate attention.

Henry repeatedly described the audit as a diagnostic process before moving into solutions.

# INTERNAL SYSTEM OUTPUT

Behind the scenes, Module 3 should generate a structured diagnosis object that Module 4 consumes.

This should include:

* Overall business health score.
* Category-by-category assessments.
* Strengths.
* Weaknesses.
* Risks.
* Opportunities.
* Priority rankings.
* Readiness classification.
* Supporting evidence from audit responses.

This structured output ensures that recommendations are generated from diagnosed business conditions rather than generic templates.

---

# Module 3 End State

At the end of Module 3, the business owner has:

* Received a comprehensive diagnosis of their business.
* Understood the current health of each major business area.
* Seen their key strengths and competitive advantages.
* Understood the most significant operational weaknesses.
* Identified the highest-priority risks.
* Recognised the greatest opportunities for improvement.
* Seen which issues require immediate attention and which can wait.
* Been prepared for the next stage, where the platform will convert these findings into tailored, budget-aware, time-aware, sector-specific **Recommended Solutions**, exactly as Henry has consistently described.

This module is deliberately **diagnostic rather than prescriptive**. It answers **"What is happening in my business?"** while leaving **"What should we do next?"** for Module 4, preserving the separation Henry repeatedly emphasized between identifying problems and recommending solutions.
